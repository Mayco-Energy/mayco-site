The problem

Electricity prices change every hour. Your machines never see it.

To produce cooling or heat below 100 °C, manufacturers and logistics operators rely on chillers and heat pumps. These machines start when the process asks for it, without looking at the hourly price or the weather.

Yet the gap between expensive and cheap hours has never been wider. Every day, part of the cooling and heat is produced at the wrong time and paid for at the highest price.

Utilities are not a site's core business. Nobody has time to re-optimise how they run, hour by hour.

The storage is already on your site.

Shifting production takes storage. On most sites, that storage already exists and needs no investment.

Goods and cold rooms

Stored products hold their cold for hours. A slight pre-cooling, within the allowed band, carries the site through the expensive hours.

Chilled-water tanks

Common in food processing, they charge with cold when power is cheap and release it to the process later.

Hot-water buffer tanks

On the heat side, buffer tanks, process vessels and even an anaerobic digester play the same reservoir role.

Method

A digital twin of your site, controlled in four steps.

From simple data, Mayco models the thermal behaviour of your site, then controls it within every one of your operating constraints.

  1. 01

    Model

    We build a stochastic digital twin of your thermal system: consumption, production, losses and external uncertainty.

    load historymachine datasheetssetpoint bands
  2. 02

    Observe

    We collect live forecasts of prices, weather and site activity, and the real state of the machines through sensors.

    day-ahead pricesweatherschedulesensors
  3. 03

    Optimise

    Our constrained optimisation algorithm computes the cheapest operating plan that respects all your limits.

    costtemperaturesmachine limits
  4. 04

    Execute

    Our on-site gateway, supervised remotely, passes setpoints to your existing controllers and reports measurements back.

    existing PLCssetpoint log

Measurements recalibrate the model. The gap between what the controller predicted and what the sensors measure keeps refining your site's digital twin.

Mayco works as a control layer on top of your installation, without replacing it.

FAQ

What site operators ask us.

Do I need to replace my machines?

No. Mayco controls the chillers and heat pumps already in place. Only a communication gateway is connected to your controllers.

Will my products stay at the right temperature?

Yes. Control stays within the temperature band you set with us, taking your regulatory and quality requirements into account. Temperatures are logged continuously.

What happens if the connection drops?

Your machines automatically return to their usual regulation. Mayco control sits on top of your installation without replacing it.

What role does my refrigeration contractor keep?

They keep the maintenance of and responsibility for your equipment. We work with them and only act through the interfaces provided by the manufacturer.

Where do the savings come from?

From several levers combined: buying electricity at the cheapest hours, running machines when their efficiency is best, limiting the power drawn at peak times, and selling your site's flexibility to the grid.

My electricity contract is not indexed to the spot price. Is control still useful?

Yes. Off-peak tariffs, contracted capacity and flexibility mechanisms also reward shifting consumption. The feasibility study quantifies what applies to your contract.

How is Mayco paid?

Through an annual subscription indexed on your savings, with a minimum fee. Savings are measured against a baseline agreed together when the contract is signed.

What happens to my data?

It is hosted in the European Union and kept separate for each customer. It is used to control your site and, in aggregated and anonymised form, to improve our models.
