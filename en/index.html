Before signing

  1. You send us what you already have: electricity bills, supervision history, machine datasheets. Nothing to install at this stage.

    Your partGather these documents.

  2. Our AI turns them into a digital twin of your thermal system: production, storage, losses, efficiency depending on the weather. It replays your history with Mayco control to cost your savings: this is the feasibility study.

    Your partApprove your temperature bands and the baseline used to measure savings.

After signing

  1. With your refrigeration contractor, we connect a communication gateway to your controllers or supervision system, and check that every setpoint is applied.

    Your partSite access and your contractor’s agreement.

  2. Control first runs without acting. The twin recalibrates on your site’s real measurements, which always differ a little from the model.

    Your partNothing: your machines run as usual.

  3. Control takes over gradually, always within your bands. Your teams are trained on fallback modes and can take back control at any time.

    Your partTraining for your operations team.

  4. Every month, a report: savings measured against the baseline, logged temperatures, time-stamped setpoints. A full review after twelve months, covering a winter and a summer.

    Your partA regular check-in with your technical manager.

FAQ

What site operators ask us.

Do I need to replace my machines?

No. Mayco controls the chillers and heat pumps already in place. Only a communication gateway is connected to your controllers.

Will my products stay at the right temperature?

Yes. Control stays within the temperature band you set with us, taking your regulatory and quality requirements into account. Temperatures are logged continuously.

What happens if the connection drops?

Your machines automatically return to their usual regulation. Mayco control sits on top of your installation without replacing it.

What role does my refrigeration contractor keep?

They keep the maintenance of and responsibility for your equipment. We work with them and only act through the interfaces provided by the manufacturer.

My electricity contract is not indexed to the spot price. Is control still useful?

Yes. Off-peak tariffs, contracted capacity and flexibility mechanisms also reward shifting consumption. The feasibility study quantifies what applies to your contract.

How is Mayco paid?

Through an annual subscription indexed on your savings, with a minimum fee. Savings are measured against a baseline agreed together when the contract is signed.

What happens to my data?

It is hosted in the European Union and kept separate for each customer. It is used to control your site and, in aggregated and anonymised form, to improve our models.
